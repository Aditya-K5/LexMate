# LexMate - Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Overview

LexMate is a mobile-first legal practice management SaaS for lawyers and law firms.

The product gives a lawyer one workspace to manage:
- Cases
- Hearings and calendar events
- Clients
- Documents
- Tasks and deadlines
- Payments and financial tracking
- Case timelines
- Notifications
- AI-assisted legal workflow

Primary platform: Android and iOS mobile app.

## 2. Problem Statement

Lawyers often manage case information, hearing dates, documents, client communication, tasks, and payments across disconnected tools.

LexMate aims to centralize these workflows so a lawyer can quickly understand:
- What needs attention today
- Which hearings are upcoming
- Which deadlines are approaching
- Which cases need action
- Which documents belong to a case
- Which clients require attention
- Which payments are pending

## 3. Goals

### MVP Goals
1. Provide a reliable case management workflow.
2. Provide hearing/calendar management with reminders.
3. Provide client management.
4. Provide secure document organization.
5. Provide task and deadline tracking.
6. Provide a useful dashboard.
7. Establish the technical foundation for AI-assisted case intelligence.
8. Support organization/team-based SaaS usage from the beginning.

### Non-goals for the first MVP
- Full legal research platform
- Automated legal advice
- Complex accounting/ERP functionality
- Fully autonomous legal decision-making

## 4. Target Users

### Primary
- Individual advocates/lawyers
- Small and medium-sized law firms
- Associates and junior lawyers

### Secondary
- Legal staff
- Firm administrators
- Clients through future client-facing workflows

## 6. AI Direction

AI is an assistant layer, not an autonomous legal decision-maker.

Planned capabilities:
- Case summarization
- Document summarization
- Ask questions about case documents
- Extract important dates
- Find relevant documents
- Generate hearing summaries
- Assist with drafting workflows

AI responses should be grounded in available case/document data where applicable, clearly distinguish generated content from source documents, and require lawyer review before use.

## 7. Product Principles

- Fast for everyday legal work
- Clear over clever
- Secure by default
- Mobile-first
- Searchable
- Auditable
- Human-controlled AI
- Consistent design system
